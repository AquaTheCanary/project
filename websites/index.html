Your free address will be convex-project.abrdns.com/your-site. Your six-digit website ID will also redirect here.

Connect a domain you own

Enter a hostname such as www.example.com. If it is in your connected ClouDNS account, Project will create the exact CNAME and certificate-validation records automatically. Otherwise, the DNS records you need will be shown here. Free Project addresses use wildcard DNS and HTTPS, so they are ready without per-site setup. Custom domains are separate: connect a domain you own, point its CNAME to the target shown, and complete the DNS verification records. Automatic HTTPS for custom domains requires the platform custom-hostname service to be configured.
